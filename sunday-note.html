A LETTER FROM COMMON GROUND / NO. 04

On keeping the good ones.

SUNDAY, SEPTEMBER 27 · 4 MIN READ

There is a mug in my cupboard with a chip on the handle. I reach for it every morning.

Not because it's precious. Not because it was expensive. It's just the right size, sits nicely in my hand, and has been along for enough early starts that it feels like mine. The chip is a little record of a life being lived.

That's the thing about everyday objects: we don't need more of them. We need a few that do their job, feel good to use, and give us no reason to send them away when the next new thing comes along.

Use it well. Let it become yours.

So here's a small Sunday invitation: pick one thing you already own and give it the attention you might give a new thing. Make the tea. Mend the loose thread. Put the flowers in the vase. Ordinary is plenty.

Take care,
Common Ground
